# GovGazette

Author: Daniel Hallman

## Quick start

1. Open [GovGazette](https://govgazette.com) to browse federal notices, awards, vendors, and exclusion records.
2. For agent use, connect `https://govgazette.com/mcp`. Public research is free and requires no account or API key.
3. Confirm that the client lists the research tools, then ask a question about a contract, award, vendor, or possible exclusion match.

## Research tools

- **Opportunities:** `search_opportunities`, `research_opportunity`, `freshness`.
- **Awards, vendors, and classifications:** `search`, `detail`, `document`, `explain_match`.
- **Exclusions:** `check_exclusions`, `search_exclusions`, `exclusion_context`, `exclusion_changes`.

Cite the GovGazette record and official source with source dates. Do not infer eligibility, incumbents, or exclusion clearance beyond the returned evidence.
