# GovGazette 1.3.4

October 28, 2026

This update makes GovGazette's setup and research instructions easier to follow.

- Follow shorter setup steps and check that the research tools are connected.
- Use clearer guidance for opportunity briefs, award and vendor research, and exclusion reviews.
- Keep source dates, exact record identifiers, and coverage limits with your findings.

The research tools, server address, and access requirements are unchanged.
Public research is free and requires no account or API key. Private saved-search
actions require an authenticated workspace and your request.

Update your installed plugin or skill package to receive the revised instructions.
Use the [setup guide](https://github.com/mooshee/govgazette-mcp#set-up-your-agent) for your agent. Existing server
connections continue to work. This package release does not establish approval
in a provider's curated directory.
